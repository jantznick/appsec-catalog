Current State

What's running today

Only the tools in play right now. Detection feeds the AI SOC; the MSSP triages and logs cases in its own ticketing system, and escalations reach the Hearst SOC. GitHub holds the Terraform that manages Sumo today, and Confluence holds the runbooks.

Not yet in play — coming soon
n8n, OpenSTI, Anansi, MISP, in-house Case Management, Caldera, Atomic Red Team, and Vector are on the roadmap. Today there's no central threat-intel fusion, no community sharing, no automated detection validation, GitHub isn't running a detection-as-code pipeline yet, and case data lives in the MSSP's ticketing system rather than our own.

The Intelligence Lifecycle

Six stages, one continuous loop

Order matters here — this is the path a single indicator travels from the outside world to a contained threat and back out as shared intel.

01 — COLLECT

Gather raw signal

MISP community events and sector feeds pull in raw indicators; Anansi pivots from them to map related attacker infrastructure.

AnansiMISP
02 — AGGREGATE & SCORE

Make sense of it

The OpenSTI normalizes formats, removes duplicates, de-conflicts sources, and scores each indicator for confidence and relevance.

OpenSTI
03 — OPERATIONALIZE

Push to defenses

High-confidence, in-scope IOCs are exported from OpenSTI into Sumo Logic and CrowdStrike as watchlists, rules, and blocks.

OpenSTISumoCrowdStrike
04 — DETECT & ESCALATE

Triage the hit

Sumo or CrowdStrike fires; Intezer auto-triages, the MSSP monitors 24/7, and only real escalations reach the Hearst SOC as a case.

Sumo / CrowdStrikeIntezerMSSP
05 — ENRICH & INVESTIGATE

Build the picture

n8n auto-enriches from OpenSTI; the case shows actor, campaign, related IOCs. The analyst pivots, scopes, and confirms against runbooks in Confluence.

Case MgmtOpenSTIConfluence
06 — RESPOND & SHARE

Contain, then give back

n8n triggers containment via CrowdStrike; confirmed observables go through OpenSTI into a MISP event shared with the community. The writeup lands in Confluence.

n8nCrowdStrikeMISP
↻ Stage 06 feeds stage 01 — every closed case makes the next detection sharper, and Caldera proves it still fires.

Why It Matters

The gaps the new tools close

Each coming-soon platform exists to fix something the SOC can't do well today. Here's the before and after.

OpenSTI

Coming soon
Gap today
Feeds are siloed, noisy, and unscored; analysts cross-reference by hand.
What it closes
One fused, scored intel picture that auto-enriches detection and cases.

Anansi

Coming soon
Gap today
Pivoting from an indicator to related infrastructure is slow and manual, with no way to see how observables connect.
What it closes
A custom, lightweight Maltego for fast OSINT pivoting and relationship mapping — built in-house.

MISP

Coming soon
Gap today
Intel is consumed but never shared; no structured IOC exchange with peers.
What it closes
Two-way sharing with ISACs and partners — give and receive vetted intel.

Case Management

Coming soon
Gap today
Case data lives in the MSSP's ticketing system — externally owned, with limited retention and real risk of losing history if we change providers.
What it closes
An in-house system of record we own, with auditable timelines and multi-year incident retention.

n8n

Coming soon
Gap today
n8n is deployed but not operationalized — enrichment and response are still manual.
What it closes
Automated playbooks that wire detection, intel, and cases together and cut response time.

Purple Team

Coming soon
Gap today
Detections are assumed to work; coverage gaps stay invisible until an incident. (Caldera + Atomic Red Team)
What it closes
Continuous ATT&CK emulation that proves detections fire and pinpoints blind spots.

Vector

Coming soon
Gap today
All logs ship raw to Sumo — high ingest cost and low signal.
What it closes
A pipeline to filter, route, and reduce before ingest — controlling spend and noise.

ANALYST DAILY WORKFLOW & SHIFT HANDOFF

Structure prevents chaos. Each shift follows a defined rhythm — open, work, hand off — so no alert is dropped and no context is lost.

🌅 MSSP — Shift Start
Review open tickets from prior shift
Check Sumo Logic alert queue
Review any Intezer auto-triage results
Acknowledge and begin working priority alerts
⚙️ MSSP — During Shift
Triage alerts: close FPs, escalate confirmed threats
n8n auto-enriches escalations with IOC context
Document findings in MSSP ticket
Escalate P1/P2 to Hearst SOC immediately
🌙 MSSP — Shift Handoff
Update all open ticket statuses
Write shift summary note in Case Management
Flag any pending decisions for Hearst SOC
Confirm no unacknowledged critical alerts
🔎 Hearst SOC — Daily
Review overnight MSSP escalations
Progress open cases in Case Management
Pull relevant runbooks from Confluence
Coordinate containment decisions with MSSP
Feed confirmed IOCs into OpenSTI
⚙️ Detection Eng — Weekly
Review FP rate report from MSSP
Tune noisy rules in Sumo Logic
Process detection backlog from hunt & TI
Run Atomic Red Team regression tests
Merge approved PRs to production
🌐 Threat Intel — Weekly
Review new feeds in OpenSTI
Score & operationalize new IOCs
Publish relevant intel to MISP
Brief Hearst SOC on active threat actors
Submit detection requests for new TTPs
