# AppSec Center of Excellence

The **AppSec Center of Excellence (ASCOE)** is a recurring, cross-company gathering for everyone participating in the AppSec program — a place to compare notes on real problems and real solutions, not another compliance meeting.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cadence** | Quarterly |
| **Hosted by** | Hearst's AppSec team |
| **Audience** | Open to anyone who wants to attend — technical practitioners and higher-level management alike |
| **Content** | Company-to-company sharing, open AppSec discussion, and vendor workshops/talks |

## How it works

- **Company-to-company sharing** — Hearst companies talk about what they're doing, problems they've run into, and how they solved them.
- **General AppSec discussion** — open conversation across the group, not a one-way presentation.
- **Vendor workshops and talks** — educational sessions from security vendors, focused on teaching general AppSec concepts, not sales pitches.
- **Kept technical where it counts** — but accessible to non-engineering attendees too.

## Why it exists

Every Hearst company works through the same lifecycle and policy baseline (see [Program Overview](/docs/program-overview)), but hits different problems along the way. The ASCOE keeps that knowledge from staying siloed — a lesson learned at one company saves the next company the same pain.

## Getting content

Session materials, recordings, and resources from past ASCOE sessions will be available through a dedicated portal being built into Orbit. Until that ships, reach out to Hearst's AppSec team directly for materials or to be added to the invite list.
