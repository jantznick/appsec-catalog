# Security Champions Program

The **Security Champions Program** is a turnkey way for a Hearst company to run its own internal security-awareness program, without having to build the content themselves.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cadence** | Monthly |
| **Hosted by** | Hearst's AppSec team (initially), then your company |
| **Audience** | Security champions — employees who volunteer as security-minded points of contact within their teams |
| **Content** | News articles, lessons learned from other Hearst companies, games, and challenges — supplied every month |

## How it works

- **Hearst's AppSec team supplies the content** — every month, a ready-to-use package for a security champions meeting.
- **Your company hosts the meeting** — using that content, with your own security champions.
- **Hearst's AppSec team runs the first few sessions** — to model the format and get the program off the ground.
- **Ownership transitions to your company** — once established, someone from your company takes over hosting the monthly meetings.
- **Support is always available** — Hearst's AppSec team helps any time after handoff, not just during rollout.

## Why it exists

Security champions programs work best when someone locally owns them — but building fresh content every month is the part that usually causes these programs to stall out. This program removes that burden: your company runs the meetings, Hearst's AppSec team keeps the content flowing.

## Getting content

Monthly content packages will be delivered through a dedicated portal being built into Orbit. Until that ships, reach out to Hearst's AppSec team directly to get set up.
